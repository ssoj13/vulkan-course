A Vulkan graphics pipeline is one object that holds the whole recipe of a draw: the shaders and the settings of every
fixed-function stage between them. This lab answers the question **what exactly goes into that object, and what can
stay outside of it?** It previews the pipeline module; the reason pipelines exist at all is in
[why Vulkan](lesson:why-vulkan#vulkan-say-everything-up-front).

The strip lists the stages in the order of the spec's block diagram. Click one to see what it does, which member of
`VkGraphicsPipelineCreateInfo` configures it, and which of its state may be **dynamic**: left out of the pipeline and
set with a `vkCmdSet*` command while recording. The list of dynamic states is generated from the Vulkan registry (`vk.xml`) and holds
only core states: 9 from Vulkan 1.0, 15 promoted in 1.3, one in 1.4. Shaders never appear there: in core Vulkan a
different shader always means a different pipeline.

Below, one triangle goes through the stages on a 16×10 framebuffer: scrub through it frame by frame. The coverage,
facing and depth rules are the spec's; the scale is tiny and there is no perspective, which the lab says on the spot.
The last part counts the pipelines of an illustrative scene with state baked in and with state made dynamic.
